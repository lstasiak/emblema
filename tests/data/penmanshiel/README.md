# Penmanshiel wind farm sample

One turbine table from each of the two archives the Penmanshiel wind farm data holds for 2016,
`Penmanshiel_SCADA_2016_WT01-10_3107.zip` (turbine 01, with its status log) and
`Penmanshiel_SCADA_2016_WT11-15_3107.zip` (turbine 15): each table's preamble and header copied
byte for byte and one of its ten-minute rows in every four days from its first, 2016-06-06 18:10
and 2016-07-14 20:00. The farm's turbines started over eight weeks and its archives split them
in two, which the sample keeps: the farm's turbines are found across archives, and the last
turbine's first month is shorter than the first one's. The status log keeps its preamble, header
and first five events and is not read. The rows are real values at their real instants, every
one of the table's 300 columns kept.

This is test data for the corpus reader. Keep the archives byte-exact: the reader's checksum is
computed over the raw bytes. They are regenerated by the script in the Kelmarsh sample's README.
