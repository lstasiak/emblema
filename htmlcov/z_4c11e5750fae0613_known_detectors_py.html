11class KnownDetectors: 

12 """The detectors every detection campaign competes, each under the name it is compared by. 

13 

14 One detector over the pretrained encoder's states, one over the same encoder at its 

15 initialisation, reading the windows as the backbone read them, one over the summaries per 

16 channel and one over their counts of readings alone: the same distance to the same number 

17 of nearest fitting windows under the same alarm, so a campaign of them reads what the 

18 representation adds and nothing else, and whether the sampling alone tells as much. Both 

19 encoders are read at every block side by side, the reading the probes found the states 

20 worth most under, and pooled by the mean over the window's tokens. Beside them, three scored 

21 by the error of what is filled in where the backbone's own masks hide a window's cells: by 

22 the backbone's pretext, by a ridge per channel over the same visible cells, and by each 

23 channel's line between its visible bins. The neighbours or the draws, the alarm's share and 

24 what a score is read against are knobs of the name, declared with the campaign. A register 

25 rather than a table read from configuration, for the reason the arms are one: the names are 

26 what a stored campaign refers to. 

27 """
