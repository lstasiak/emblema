63class CompositionRoot: 

64 """Assembles the HTTP process: what it answers with, and what it runs on. 

65 

66 Two contexts answer here. Serving answers predictions and representations and shows what it 

67 serves; Evaluation shows its campaigns. Both read one database and one store, so both are 

68 composed on one engine and one store, and neither feeds the other: what is promotable is 

69 decided where campaigns close, and this process closes none. 

70 

71 Nothing of the training stack is composed in: a network is run through its graph, a 

72 classical candidate through the service the Evaluation context publishes for it, and the 

73 tokenisation of a request through the service the Catalog publishes. All lifetimes are 

74 process-scoped, the loaded candidates and the verdicts read included, which is the point of 

75 a service that answers many requests with the same few models. 

76 

77 Attributes: 

78 adapters: The port implementations, and the published services, the process runs on. 

79 services: The use cases, each already holding its dependencies. 

80 readiness: What the readiness probe asks: the database and the store unless given. 

81 """
