4class TransferMode(StrEnum): 

5 """What the backbone's weights do while a task is learnt from a budget of labels. 

6 

7 One axis of the label-efficiency curve, the control arm included: every member is the same 

8 architecture answering the same task through the same head, and they differ only in which 

9 weights are trained and where they start. Keeping the control on the axis is what makes a 

10 cell of the curve one comparison rather than two. 

11 

12 Attributes: 

13 FROM_SCRATCH: The architecture randomly initialised, every weight trained. The control 

14 arm: what the labels teach on their own. 

15 FROZEN_PROBE: The pretrained weights held fixed and a linear head trained over the pooled 

16 states. What the representation carries as it stands. 

17 LORA: The pretrained weights held fixed, low-rank updates trained beside chosen linear 

18 layers together with the head. At this model size not an economy of memory or time 

19 but a cap on the degrees of freedom a small budget may spend. 

20 FULL_FINE_TUNING: The pretrained weights, every one trained together with the head. 

21 """ 

22 

23 FROM_SCRATCH = "from_scratch" 

24 FROZEN_PROBE = "frozen_probe" 

25 LORA = "lora" 

26 FULL_FINE_TUNING = "full_fine_tuning"
