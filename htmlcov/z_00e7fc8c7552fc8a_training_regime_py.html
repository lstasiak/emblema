10class ClassWeight(StrEnum): 

11 """How the two outcomes of a binary task weigh in the loss. 

12 

13 Attributes: 

14 NONE: Every window weighs the same, as every campaign ran before this was a knob. 

15 RATIO: The positive outcome weighs the ratio of negatives to positives in the labels 

16 learnt from, so each class contributes alike; published networks on the 

17 intensive-care task weight this way. 

18 """ 

19 

20 NONE = "none" 

21 RATIO = "ratio"

24@dataclass(frozen=True, kw_only=True) 

25class TrainingRegime: 

26 """How a run is stopped, weighted and perturbed while it learns, beyond its schedule. 

27 

28 The schedule says how long a run may take and at what rate; the regime says what else is 

29 done inside that budget. The standard regime is the one every campaign ran under before 

30 these were knobs: every epoch of the schedule, the last weights kept, every window weighing 

31 the same, every channel read. The three parts here are the ones published networks on 

32 irregular clinical series train under and this project's did not, tried together because, 

33 turned one at a time inside such a network, they cost it little, and turned together a lot. 

34 

35 A stop holds out a share of the labelled sample, by unit, and keeps the weights of the epoch 

36 that scored best on it, giving up after ``patience`` epochs without a better one. The 

37 validation side is never read for it, so what is reported there stays what it was: the held 

38 out units are taken from the labels the run was given, and the run learns from fewer. The 

39 schedule's epochs become a cap, so a stopped run spends at most the budget it was declared 

40 under, never more. 

41 

42 A variant turns the stop's two knobs one at a time, so a regime may hold one of them while 

43 the other is being turned; the run stops only once both are stated, and a plan refuses a 

44 stop stated in part. 

45 

46 Invariants: the share held out lies in ``[0, 0.5]``; the patience is not negative; the 

47 channel dropout lies in ``[0, 1)``. 

48 

49 Attributes: 

50 stop_share: Share of the labelled units held out to stop on; zero for no stop. 

51 patience: Epochs without a better score on the held-out units before the run stops; 

52 zero for no stop. 

53 class_weight: How the outcomes weigh in the loss. 

54 channel_dropout: Probability a window's channel is withheld whole while it is learnt 

55 from; zero for every channel read. 

56 """ 

57 

58 stop_share: float = 0.0 

59 patience: int = 0 

60 class_weight: ClassWeight = ClassWeight.NONE 

61 channel_dropout: float = 0.0

63 KNOBS: ClassVar[tuple[str, ...]] = ("stop_share", "patience", "class_weight", "channel_dropout")

92 def tuned(self, knob: str, value: str) -> Self: 

93 """This regime with ``knob`` turned to ``value``. 

94 

95 Raises: 

96 UnknownKnobError: If the regime has no such knob, or it cannot take that value. 

97 """ 

98 if knob not in self.KNOBS: 

99 raise UnknownKnobError(f"a regime has no knob {knob!r}; it turns {self.KNOBS}") 

100 if knob == "class_weight": 

101 try: 

102 weight = ClassWeight(value) 

103 except ValueError as error: 

104 raise UnknownKnobError(f"class_weight cannot be {value}: {error}") from error 

105 return turned(self, knob, weight) 

106 return turned(self, knob, value)

110 return {

118 """Only the knobs turned away from the standard regime, for a candidate's description. 

119 

120 Left unsaid where standard for the reason the encoder's setting leaves its standard 

121 values unsaid: the descriptions stored before these were knobs must stay what they were.

123 turned_knobs: dict[str, float | int | str] = {} 

124 if self.stop_share > 0.0:
