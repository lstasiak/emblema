45class RunClassicalFit: 

46 """Fits a candidate that starts from no weights on one budget of labels, and scores it. 

47 

48 The counterpart of adapting a backbone, drawing the same labels from the same side under the 

49 same seed, so that a cell of the grid means the same thing whichever kind of candidate stood 

50 in it. What differs is the source tasks: a recipe that names them is fitted on their labels 

51 as well, which is how a classical method answers the question about transfer. 

52 

53 Every source contributes a budget of the same size, drawn under its own task's strata and 

54 under the run's seed. That it is the same size is a decision, not an omission: a second dial 

55 for how much a source contributes would be a dial nobody recorded and everybody could turn 

56 after seeing the numbers, so what a transfer candidate saw follows from the cell's 

57 coordinates and from the tasks the recipe names. 

58 """
