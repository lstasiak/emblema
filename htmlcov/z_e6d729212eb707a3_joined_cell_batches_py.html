18 """Cells of windows joined with those before them: masks over the full cells, then rejoined. 

19 

20 A batch is joined from every window's cells first, which gives every cell of the joined 

21 window, empty ones included, for the masks to be drawn over and for a hidden cell's target, 

22 and the layout keeps a row for each channel the joined window observes, as a layout over a 

23 window's own channels does. Once the masks are drawn the hidden cells are emptied in the 

24 cells joined from and the windows joined again on the same rows, so a cell after a hidden one 

25 carries forward and counts from what the encoder can see, as laying the readings without the 

26 hidden ones would give. 

27

29 joined: How the cells of a window's span are joined into its bins. 

30 """
