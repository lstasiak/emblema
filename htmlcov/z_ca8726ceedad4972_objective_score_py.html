7class ObjectiveScore: 

8 """What one batch cost an objective, before it is divided: three sums over the same tokens. 

9 

10 The error carries the graph the step is taken on; the trivial predictor's loss is what the 

11 same tokens would have cost a model that learnt nothing, read the same way, and is detached; 

12 the count is what divides either into a mean. Summed rather than averaged so that whoever 

13 accumulates several batches into one gradient adds the sums and divides once, and a batch 

14 that hid three tokens weighs a hundredth of one that hid three hundred. 

15 

16 Attributes: 

17 error: The run's loss summed over the hidden tokens that were observed. 

18 trivial: The trivial predictor's loss summed over the same tokens. 

19 tokens: How many tokens were scored. 

20 """
