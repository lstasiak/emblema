8class BackboneFactory(Protocol): 

9 """Where this context's torch adapters get an encoder from, without knowing whose it is. 

10 

11 A seam of the adapter, not a port of the hexagon: no use case sees it, and it speaks torch, 

12 which a port may not. Evaluation may not import the context that owns the encoder, so the 

13 process that composes the adapter implements this. A backbone is any module that takes the 

14 five tensors of a batch in the order ``TokenTensors.args`` gives them and returns one state 

15 per token, ``[batch, tokens, width]``; it is built on the host and moved by the runtime. 

16 

17 Every encoder is asked for over the vocabulary of the task's corpus, because a corpus 

18 published under a vocabulary that continues the backbone's names channels the backbone never 

19 saw: a pretrained encoder grows rows for them, and the modules holding grown rows say so 

20 through ``GrownParameters``, so the adapter can train them under every mode. 

21 """
