4class ScoreReference(StrEnum): 

5 """What a detector's distance is read against before the alarm is applied. 

6 

7 A detector fitted once on the fitting side reads every later window against that one 

8 standard, so a recording that drifts away from it raises alarms everywhere and the events 

9 are lost among them. Reading each window against the other windows of its own unit instead 

10 asks how unusual it is for that month or that experiment; no label is spent, since the unit's 

11 windows arrive unlabelled, and a window alone in its unit has nothing to be read against. 

12 

13 Attributes: 

14 FITTING_SIDE: The distance itself, every window held to the standard the fit set. 

15 OWN_UNIT: The distance less the median of its unit's distances, over their spread 

16 (the median absolute deviation, scaled to a standard deviation), the fitting side's 

17 windows read the same way within their own units when the alarm is set. 

18 """ 

19 

20 FITTING_SIDE = "fitting" 

21 OWN_UNIT = "unit"
