12@dataclass(frozen=True, kw_only=True) 

13class PromotableArtifact: 

14 """An artifact a finished campaign kept, as Serving knows it: the only thing it may promote. 

15 

16 A projection of what Evaluation announced, not a reference into its tables. It is built 

17 from the message a campaign publishes when it finishes, so the promise that a served model 

18 was measured holds without a foreign key across schemas and without asking the other 

19 context anything when a model is promoted. One exists only for a competitor whose artifact 

20 the campaign kept; the others have nothing to serve. 

21 

22 Every kind of candidate is promotable on the same terms, which is why the kind is carried and 

23 never consulted here: it says which runtime loads the artifact, not whether it may be loaded. 

24 The standing and the scores are recorded for whoever chooses, and are not a condition either, 

25 for the reason ``CampaignScore`` gives. 

26 

27 Invariants: no operating point — a metric at a budget — is scored twice. 

28 

29 Attributes: 

30 origin: The campaign, task and competitor the artifact was measured as. 

31 kind: What the candidate is made of, which decides what can run the artifact. 

32 artifact: The fitted candidate, by reference and checksum. 

33 standing: Where the candidate ended against the campaign's control. 

34 scores: What it scored, one figure per operating point. 

35 completed_at: When the campaign finished, which is when the artifact became promotable. 

36 """ 

37
