7@dataclass(frozen=True, kw_only=True) 

8class GradientBoostingSpec: 

9 """How many trees a classical candidate grows, how deep, and how much each one is trusted. 

10 

11 Stated as a value rather than left to whatever the library defaults to, for the reason every 

12 other knob in this project is stated: a baseline tuned by its defaults is a baseline nobody 

13 can repeat, and a comparison against it says as much about the release of a package as about 

14 the method. The counts are the whole of what a fit costs, so they are also what a campaign 

15 records when it records what this candidate was set to. 

16 

17 The threads are here rather than in the environment that happens to run the fit, because 

18 they are part of the answer and not a setting of convenience: a histogram is summed in the 

19 order the work was split in, so two machines agree on what a recipe produced only if they 

20 agree on this. A campaign that recorded every other knob and left this one to whichever 

21 worker picked the cell up would record a recipe that does not identify its own result. 

22 

23 Invariants: at least one round, one level of depth and one thread; the rate is positive and 

24 finite; the two shares lie in ``(0, 1]``; the leaf weight and the penalty are finite and not 

25 negative. 

26 

27 Attributes: 

28 rounds: Trees grown, one per boosting round. 

29 max_depth: Levels a tree may reach. 

30 learning_rate: Share of each tree's correction that is kept. 

31 row_share: Rows drawn for each tree, as a share of the fitted windows. 

32 feature_share: Features offered to each tree, as a share of the vector's width. 

33 min_leaf_weight: Least total weight a leaf may hold, which for a squared-error fit is a 

34 count of windows. 

35 l2_penalty: Penalty on the leaf values, which keeps a leaf standing on few windows from 

36 taking a large one. 

37 threads: How many threads a fit may use, which is part of what it answers. 

38 """
