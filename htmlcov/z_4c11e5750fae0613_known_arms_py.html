10class KnownArms: 

11 """The four ways of using a backbone, each under the name a campaign competes it by. 

12 

13 What each arm does with the weights is fixed — that is what the name means — while the 

14 weights themselves, the strength of the low-rank update and the schedule are a campaign's 

15 choice, so all three come in when the process is told what it serves. A register rather than 

16 a table read from configuration, because the names are what a stored campaign refers to: an 

17 arm renamed in an environment file would leave a finished grid naming candidates nothing 

18 supplies. 

19 """
