8class KnownArms: 

9 """The four ways of using a backbone, each under the name a campaign competes it by. 

10 

11 What each arm does with the weights is fixed — that is what the name means — while the 

12 weights themselves and the strength of the low-rank update are a campaign's choice, so both 

13 come in when the process is told what it serves. A register rather than a table read from 

14 configuration, because the names are what a stored campaign refers to: an arm renamed in an 

15 environment file would leave a finished grid naming candidates nothing supplies. 

16 """
