9@dataclass(frozen=True, kw_only=True) 

10class EncoderReading: 

11 """Which frozen encoder a detector reads a window through, and how its states are pooled. 

12 

13 A detector reads an encoder as a probe does, once per window and with nothing trained: the 

14 encoder is the pretrained one or the same shape at its initialisation, read at the layer the 

15 setting names, and the states per token are pooled by a scheme with no weights of its own, 

16 since nothing here learns. 

17 

18 Invariants: the pooling learns no weights; the encoder drops nothing, since it never learns; 

19 the readings are the tokens as the encoder was trained on them, neither gridded, clipped 

20 nor replaced by the summaries, and a pretrained encoder keeps its own build. 

21 

22 Attributes: 

23 architecture: Artifact of the model whose shape the encoder has, whether it starts from 

24 its weights or draws them anew. 

25 backbone: Artifact of the pretrained weights; ``None`` for the shape at its 

26 initialisation. 

27 encoder: Which readings the encoder is given and which layer it is read at. 

28 pooling: How the states of a window become the one state the detector reads. 

29 """ 

30 

31 architecture: ArtifactRef 

32 backbone: ArtifactRef | None 

33 encoder: EncoderSetting = field(default_factory=EncoderSetting.standard) 

34 pooling: HeadPooling = field(default_factory=HeadPooling.mean)

38 raise InvalidDetectorRecipeError(

42 raise InvalidDetectorRecipeError("a detector learns nothing, so it drops nothing") 

43 if self.encoder.grid_resolution is not None or self.encoder.value_clip is not None:
