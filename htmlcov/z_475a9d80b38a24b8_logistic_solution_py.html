16@dataclass(frozen=True) 

17class LogisticSolution: 

18 """A linear head over pooled states answering an outcome's log-odds, by penalised likelihood. 

19 

20 The head a network over outcomes learns by binary cross-entropy, solved to its optimum 

21 instead of stepped towards it: logistic regression with an unpenalised intercept and a 

22 penalty of half the given strength on the squared weights, the columns scaled by their 

23 spread as the least-squares head's are. The answer is the log-odds itself, so the head 

24 ranks the windows by the fit and a sigmoid reads it as a probability, with nothing fitted 

25 afterwards that could reorder or flatten it. 

26 

27 The penalty is the one whose heads, fitted without each of ``OutcomeFolds`` in turn, give 

28 the folds the smallest mean log-loss, the smaller one on a tie; the head is then fitted on 

29 every window. The optimiser is Newton's method with a backtracking line search, in double 

30 precision on the host, stopped once half the squared Newton decrement — a bound on how far 

31 the objective still is from its minimum — falls below the tolerance, and finished with one 

32 full step. 

33 

34 Attributes: 

35 weights: The linear map over the states as they were given, one weight per column. 

36 intercept: What the map adds. 

37 penalty: The penalty the fit chose. 

38 held_out_loss: Mean log-loss over the folds under each penalty, in their order. 

39 """ 

40 

41 weights: Tensor 

42 intercept: float 

43 penalty: float 

44 held_out_loss: tuple[float, ...] 

45 

46 _TOLERANCE: ClassVar[float] = 1e-10 

47 _ITERATIONS: ClassVar[int] = 100 

48 # A step is accepted when it buys at least this share of the decrease its gradient promises, 

49 # halved down to the smallest step. 

50 _ARMIJO: ClassVar[float] = 1e-4 

51 _SMALLEST_STEP: ClassVar[float] = 1e-10

53 @classmethod 

54 def fitted(cls, states: Tensor, outcomes: Tensor, penalties: RidgePenalties) -> Self: 

55 """The head over ``states`` answering ``outcomes``, choosing among ``penalties``. 

56 

57 Raises: 

58 UnfoldableOutcomesError: If an outcome is not zero or one, or either has fewer than 

59 two windows. 

60 UnsolvableHeadError: If a fit does not converge. 

61 """ 

62 x = states.detach().to("cpu").to(torch.float64) 

63 y = outcomes.detach().to("cpu").to(torch.float64) 

64 folds = OutcomeFolds.of(y.tolist()) 

65 spread = x.std(dim=0, correction=0) 

66 scale = torch.where(spread > 0.0, spread, torch.ones_like(spread)) 

67 scaled = x / scale 

68 losses: list[float] = [] 

69 for penalty in penalties.values: 

70 per_fold: list[float] = [] 

71 for fold in range(folds.count): 

72 kept, held = list(folds.kept(fold)), list(folds.held_out(fold)) 

73 weights, intercept = cls._maximised(scaled[kept], y[kept], penalty) 

74 answered = scaled[held] @ weights + intercept 

75 per_fold.append(float((softplus(answered) - y[held] * answered).mean())) 

76 losses.append(fmean(per_fold)) 

77 chosen = min(range(len(losses)), key=lambda index: (losses[index], index)) 

78 weights, intercept = cls._maximised(scaled, y, penalties.values[chosen]) 

79 return cls( 

80 weights=weights / scale, 

81 intercept=intercept, 

82 penalty=penalties.values[chosen], 

83 held_out_loss=tuple(losses), 

84 )

92 @classmethod

95

134 @staticmethod 

135 def _objective(design: Tensor, y: Tensor, shrunk: Tensor, theta: Tensor) -> float: 

136 """Negative log-likelihood plus the penalty, computed without overflow at any log-odds.""" 

137 answered = design @ theta 

138 return float((softplus(answered) - y * answered).sum() + 0.5 * (shrunk * theta**2).sum())
