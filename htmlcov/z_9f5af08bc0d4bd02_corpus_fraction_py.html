7@dataclass(frozen=True, kw_only=True) 

8class CorpusFraction: 

9 """How much of one corpus's training side a mixed run reads, where it differs from the rest. 

10 

11 A mixture of corpora of very different sizes is not read whole on every side: a corpus of a 

12 billion values is read in part and a corpus of a million in full, or the small one is a 

13 rounding error of every epoch. The fraction stated here replaces, for its corpus alone, the 

14 share the configuration states for the mixture; a corpus left unstated is read at that share, 

15 so a run without this knob is the run it always was. 

16 

17 Invariants: the corpus is named without surrounding whitespace; the fraction lies in 

18 ``(0, 1]`` — a whole corpus may be stated, where the mixture's share is less than whole. 

19 

20 Attributes: 

21 corpus: The corpus, as the experiment names it. 

22 fraction: Share of its training units the run reads. 

23 """ 

24 

25 corpus: str 

26 fraction: float 

27 

28 def __post_init__(self) -> None: 

29 if not self.corpus or self.corpus != self.corpus.strip(): 

30 raise InvalidCorpusFractionError("corpus must be named without surrounding whitespace") 

31 if not isfinite(self.fraction) or not 0.0 < self.fraction <= 1.0: 

32 raise InvalidCorpusFractionError(f"fraction must lie in (0, 1], got {self.fraction}")
