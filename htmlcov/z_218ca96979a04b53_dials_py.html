6class Dials(BaseModel): 

7 """Settings of the synthetic control: frozen, constraining each other, derived by turning one. 

8 

9 A model rather than a dataclass, because the fields constrain each other and a value that 

10 breaks a constraint must not be constructible. Derivation goes through validation for the 

11 same reason: a layout derived from a valid one is not automatically valid itself, so 

12 ``with_dials`` revalidates where ``model_copy`` would wave the constraints through. A dial 

13 the model does not have is refused for the same reason: a misspelt one would otherwise 

14 return the settings untouched, and a derivation that changed nothing reads as one that 

15 worked. 

16 

17 The presets themselves stay where they are stated. This is only the shape they share. 

18 """
