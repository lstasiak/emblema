12class ClassicalRuntime(Protocol): 

13 """Fits a candidate that starts from nothing learnt, and scores it on the windows it is given. 

14 

15 The counterpart of the runtime that adapts a backbone, and separate from it because the two 

16 describe a run by different values: one by a transfer mode over named weights, the other by 

17 a feature scheme over a fit that began empty. A port that took both would have to carry the 

18 fields of each and let an adapter ignore half of them, and a campaign reading such a record 

19 could not tell what a candidate was actually set to. 

20 

21 Nothing of a corpus crosses the port: the task names the manifest its windows sit behind and 

22 the adapter reads them. Labels of other tasks arrive as sources, already drawn and already 

23 paired with the task they belong to, so that a fit spanning corpora is a thing the caller 

24 declared rather than something an adapter decided to do. 

25 """
