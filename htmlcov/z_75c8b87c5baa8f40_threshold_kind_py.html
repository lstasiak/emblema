4class ThresholdKind(StrEnum): 

5 """How a registered threshold on a reduction is stated: as a share, or in the error's unit. 

6 

7 A share of the control's error is scale-free, which suits an error with no natural bound: 

8 a tenth of the remaining life missed reads the same on any turbofan. An error bounded by a 

9 perfect answer is different — a share of one minus an area under the ROC curve is a share of 

10 the distance left to perfect ranking, so the same share asks twice as much of a candidate 

11 against a weak control as against a strong one. Where the literature states its differences 

12 in the measure's own unit, a registration that wants to be read beside it does too. 

13 

14 Attributes: 

15 RELATIVE: A share of the control's error. 

16 ABSOLUTE: An amount in the error's own unit. 

17 """ 

18 

19 RELATIVE = "relative" 

20 ABSOLUTE = "absolute"
