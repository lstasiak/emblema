10@dataclass(frozen=True) 

11class OneStandardErrorRule: 

12 """Chooses the variant closest to its default among those as good as the best within error. 

13 

14 The rule of one standard error (Breiman, Friedman, Olshen and Stone, 1984; Hastie, 

15 Tibshirani and Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, §7.10). The lowest mean 

16 error alone would often choose noise: with a few dozen units, the spread between repeats is 

17 as large as the differences between variants. So every variant whose mean is within one 

18 standard error of the best one's counts as as good, and among those the one that departs 

19 least from the candidate's default is chosen — the default wins unless the data say 

20 clearly otherwise. 

21 

22 The repeats divide the same units over and over, so their errors are not independent, and 

23 the plain standard error would understate how uncertain the best mean is. The variance is 

24 scaled as Nadeau and Bengio (2003) correct it for repeated random holdout: by 

25 ``1 / J + n_test / n_train`` over ``J`` repeats, rather than by ``1 / J``. 

26 

27 Ties in closeness are broken by the lower mean and then by name, so a choice never depends 

28 on the order the variants were listed in. 

29 """ 

30

37 """The variant this rule chooses. 

38 

39 Args: 

40 errors: Each variant's error in every repeat, the same repeats for each. 

41 closeness: How far each variant departs from the default: how many knobs it turns, 

42 then how far, as the method states it; the default is ``(0, 0.0)``. 

43 test_to_train: Units scored for every unit learnt from, in each repeat. 

44 

45 Raises: 

46 SelectionNotReadableError: If a variant has fewer than two repeats, the variants 

47 have different repeats, or a variant's closeness is unknown. 

48 """

56 means = {variant: fmean(values) for variant, values in errors.items()} 

57 best = min(means, key=lambda variant: (means[variant], str(variant))) 

58 spread = errors[best] 

59 reach = means[best] + sqrt(variance(spread) * (1.0 / len(spread) + test_to_train)) 

60 within = [variant for variant, mean in means.items() if mean <= reach] 

61 return min(within, key=lambda variant: (closeness[variant], means[variant], str(variant)))
