4class DetectorFeatures(StrEnum): 

5 """What a detector reads a window as, before it measures how unlike the normal windows it is. 

6 

7 Attributes: 

8 ENCODER_STATES: The states of a frozen encoder pooled over the window, pretrained or 

9 drawn at its initialisation, read at the layer and under the pooling a plan names. 

10 SUMMARIES: The statistics of every channel of the window, the table the classical 

11 methods read; the bar a representation is held to. 

12 READING_COUNTS: How many readings each channel of the window holds, nothing of their 

13 values: what the sampling alone tells, the bar a detector has to pass to show it 

14 reads more than when the telemetry was sent. 

15 PRETEXT_ERROR: The error of the values a pretrained backbone's pretext fills in where 

16 its own masks hide the window's cells. 

17 LINEAR_ANSWER_ERROR: The error of the values a ridge per channel fills in from the same 

18 visible cells under the same masks, fitted on the fitting side: the answer that 

19 learnt nothing from the backbone, held to what the backbone sees. 

20 INTERPOLATION_ERROR: The error of the values each channel's visible bins interpolate 

21 under the same masks, beside the ridge. 

22 """
