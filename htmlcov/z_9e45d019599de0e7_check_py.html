7class Area(Enum): 

8 """Where a failed rule sends someone to fix it. 

9 

10 Attributes: 

11 IMPLEMENTATION: A correct pipeline cannot fail the rule: the loss that does not fall, a kind 

12 of mask the strategy draws and the verdict never sees, a model below the measurement 

13 noise (hidden values reach it, or the loss scores what was not hidden), a model no 

14 better than the channel mean on every kind at once. Failing it means changing the logic. 

15 TRAINING: The run cannot be read as it stands: whether a longer one would change the 

16 verdicts is unknown or known to be yes, the last epoch is off its best, or the model 

17 memorises. 

18 MASKING: A kind of mask teaches nothing its trivial baseline did not know. Where the 

19 baseline already sits at the noise floor that is a fact about the kind on this corpus 

20 and no training changes it; where it leaves room, a run that has not shown it stopped 

21 learning may still beat it, and the verdict waits for one that has. 

22 DIAGNOSTIC: The measurement cannot answer its own question on this corpus. 

23 EVIDENCE: Too few units or tokens for the verdict to mean anything. 

24 """ 

25

27 TRAINING = "training" 

28 MASKING = "masking" 

29 DIAGNOSTIC = "diagnostic"

84class Check: 

85 """One rule applied to one run. 

86

88 rule: The question asked.
