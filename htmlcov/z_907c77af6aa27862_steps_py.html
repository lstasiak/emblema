1"""Which step of an equal grid over a window an instant falls in, stated once for every grid. 

2 

3A rule shared by the grid that lays a window out as an array and the one that hands it back as 

4tokens, so a reading lands in the same step whichever form a method reads. 

5""" 

6 

7import numpy as np 

8from numpy.typing import NDArray 

9 

10# A token's time is stored in single precision, so a reading at the start of a step can land a 

11# hair before it — k / 50 is 0.0199999995 — and a floor would put it one step early, leaving its 

12# own step empty and doubling the one before. A tolerance far above the rounding of single 

13# precision and far below any spacing of readings puts every reading back. 

14TOLERANCE = 1e-3 

15 

16 

17def steps_of(times: NDArray[np.float64], steps: int) -> NDArray[np.int64]: 

18 """The step each of ``times``, fractions of the window, falls in; the end in the last.""" 

19 return np.minimum(np.floor(times * steps + TOLERANCE).astype(np.int64), steps - 1)
