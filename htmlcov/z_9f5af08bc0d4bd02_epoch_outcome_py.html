9@dataclass(frozen=True, kw_only=True) 

10class EpochOutcome: 

11 """What one epoch measured and what it left in the artifact store. 

12 

13 Three references rather than one, because the artifacts are kept for different reasons and 

14 for different lengths of time: a checkpoint carries the whole state of the run and exists so 

15 that a dropped session can be picked up; the weights of an epoch are written when it is the 

16 best the run has seen by its validation, so that the epoch worth keeping is kept whatever 

17 comes after it; and the backbone is the weights the run ends with — the best epoch's — which 

18 appears on the last epoch of a run and nowhere else, which is what lets a caller consuming 

19 the epochs one by one know when it has them all. 

20 

21 Invariants: the epoch is not negative; the training loss is finite and not negative; the 

22 time taken is not negative; the share hidden lies in ``(0, 1]``, since an epoch that hid 

23 nothing scored nothing; at least one corpus was validated and none of them twice. 

24 

25 Attributes: 

26 epoch: Which epoch this was, counted from zero. 

27 training_loss: The run's reading over the epoch's hidden tokens. 

28 validation: What each corpus of the held-out side cost, one entry per corpus, beside the 

29 trivial predictor's loss over the same tokens. A run over one corpus reports one 

30 entry; the mixture reports one each, because a single number over all of them is the 

31 largest corpus's number. 

32 hidden_ratio: Share of the validation windows' observed tokens those masks hid, counted 

33 rather than taken from what the strategy expects. 

34 seconds: Wall-clock time the epoch took, training and scoring together. 

35 checkpoint: The last resumable state written during the epoch; ``None`` where the policy 

36 asked for none. 

37 weights: The weights as this epoch left them, written because the epoch is the best the 

38 run has seen; ``None`` for an epoch that did not improve on an earlier one. 

39 backbone: The weights the run kept, on its final epoch only: those of the epoch whose 

40 validation was lowest, which may be an earlier one or one a resumed run inherited. 

41 """ 

42 

43 epoch: int 

44 training_loss: float 

45 validation: tuple[CorpusValidation, ...] 

46 hidden_ratio: float 

47 seconds: float 

48 checkpoint: ArtifactRef | None = None 

49 weights: ArtifactRef | None = None 

50 backbone: ArtifactRef | None = None

73 @property 

74 def validation_loss(self) -> float: 

75 """The loss over every hidden validation token, whichever corpus it came from. 

76 

77 Derived from the corpora rather than reported beside them, so that the number a single 

78 curve is drawn from and the numbers per corpus cannot disagree. It weighs a corpus by the 

79 tokens it contributed, which is what a loss over the whole side is. 

80 """ 

81 tokens = sum(scored.tokens for scored in self.validation) 

82 return sum(scored.loss * scored.tokens for scored in self.validation) / tokens 

83 

84 @property 

85 def relative_validation(self) -> float: 

86 """The mean over corpora of what each cost against nothing to learn on it. 

87 

88 What a run that stops on its validation reads, and what keeps the best epoch: a mean over 

89 corpora rather than over tokens, so that no corpus decides the number by the size of its 

90 values or the count of its windows. 

91 """ 

92 return sum(scored.relative for scored in self.validation) / len(self.validation)
