10class PoolingScheme(StrEnum): 

11 """How the states of a window's tokens become the one state a head reads. 

12 

13 Every scheme here is free of the channel layout: it weighs tokens by where they sit in the 

14 window or by what they hold, never by which channel they came from, so a head under any of 

15 them reads a corpus of eight channels as it reads one of forty-four. A pooling laid out per 

16 channel would be as wide as the corpus and is left to the classical baselines. 

17 

18 Attributes: 

19 MEAN: The mean over every observed token of the window. 

20 TAIL: The mean over the observed tokens in the last share of the window, the window's 

21 static features included, since a static feature is current at every instant. 

22 ATTENTION: A weighted mean whose weights a learnt query reads off the states themselves, 

23 so the head can learn which part of the window to read from. 

24 """ 

25 

26 MEAN = "mean" 

27 TAIL = "tail" 

28 ATTENTION = "attention" 

29 

30 @property 

31 def learns_weights(self) -> bool: 

32 """Whether the pooling has weights of its own that a run trains.""" 

33 return self is PoolingScheme.ATTENTION

36class StaticsPlacement(StrEnum): 

37 """Where a window's static features stand in the state a head reads. 

38 

39 A static feature (age, sex, the ward) is one token against the hundreds of readings a stay 

40 holds, so in the pooled state it weighs as one reading among them. Set apart, the readings 

41 are pooled by the scheme and the static features by their own mean, and the head reads the 

42 two side by side, as networks published on these stays read their demographics. Neither 

43 placement reads the channel layout: a window without static features gives zeros on their 

44 side. 

45 

46 Attributes: 

47 AMONG: Pooled with the readings, as every network was before this was a knob. 

48 APART: Pooled on their own and set beside the readings, doubling the state's width. 

49 """ 

50 

51 AMONG = "among" 

52 APART = "apart"

55@dataclass(frozen=True, kw_only=True) 

56class HeadPooling: 

57 """The pooling of a network's head: which scheme, what a tail keeps, where statics stand. 

58 

59 Stated once for every network a campaign compares, so a set encoder and a patch model pool 

60 by one vocabulary and a variant name turns the same knobs on both. A share under a scheme 

61 that reads none would be a knob recorded as turned and changing nothing, so it is refused 

62 rather than normalised. A tail over the whole window computes the mean and is allowed: a 

63 variant turns one knob at a time, and the tail has to exist before its share is set. Set 

64 apart, the static features leave whichever scheme pools the readings, a tail included. 

65 

66 Invariants: the share lies in ``(0, 1]``, and is one unless the scheme is a tail. 

67 

68 Attributes: 

69 pooling: Which scheme. 

70 tail_share: Share of the window's length a tail keeps, from its end; one otherwise. 

71 statics: Where the static features stand: among the readings unless a variant turns it. 

72 """ 

73 

74 pooling: PoolingScheme 

75 tail_share: float = 1.0 

76 statics: StaticsPlacement = StaticsPlacement.AMONG

78 KNOBS: ClassVar[tuple[str, ...]] = ("pooling", "tail_share", "statics")
