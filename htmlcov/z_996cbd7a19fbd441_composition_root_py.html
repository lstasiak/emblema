29class CompositionRoot: 

30 """Assembles the worker that adapts a backbone: the campaign process plus the arms it serves. 

31 

32 The process is told which backbone it serves, holds it, and refuses a cell of a campaign 

33 that was run over other weights. Holding it is the reason this root exists apart from the 

34 one that fits trees: reading a backbone out of the store for each of a grid's cells would 

35 download it eighty times to save keeping one copy, and everything else the two processes 

36 need is the same and is assembled once. 

37 

38 This is the image with the machine-learning stack in it. Whether a cell computes on an 

39 accelerator is the device's business and not this process's: the target platform is a 

40 container that has none, and the same entry point on a host that does is a way of reaching 

41 one rather than a second architecture. 

42 

43 Attributes: 

44 adapters: The port implementations the process runs on. 

45 services: The use cases, each already holding its dependencies. 

46 """
